## A+ Content: Your Conversion Engine

Standard Amazon listings get bullet points and a description block. Brand-registered sellers get A+ Content — a modular visual layout with comparison charts, brand stories, image carousels, and rich text modules.

The data on A+ Content is consistent across categories: listings with well-built A+ Content show 5-10% higher conversion rates than equivalent listings without it. The reason is straightforward — buyers on Amazon make split-second trust judgments based on listing quality. A listing with A+ Content signals "this brand invested in their presence here." A listing without it signals "this is a reseller or a commodity product."

Build your A+ Content based on buyer objections. Pull your competitors' negative reviews. What are buyers worried about? Durability? Size? Material quality? Each module of your A+ Content should address one specific objection with visual evidence.

## Brand Analytics: Data You Cannot Get Anywhere Else

Brand Analytics is the most underused tool in Brand Registry. It gives you:

- Search Frequency Rank for every keyword in your category (not just the ones you bid on)
- Market Basket Analysis (what products do buyers purchase together with yours?)
- Repeat Purchase Behavior (what percentage of your customers come back?)
- Demographics (age, income, education of your buyers)

None of this data is available through third-party tools. It is first-party Amazon data, available only to brand-registered sellers. Use it to inform product development, cross-selling strategy, and advertising audience targeting.

## Brand Store: Your Amazon Homepage

A Brand Store is a multi-page brand destination on Amazon. It costs nothing to build. Most sellers build a single page with their logo and a grid of products — then wonder why nobody visits.

A high-performing Brand Store has: a hero section with your brand value proposition (not just your logo), merchandise by use case instead of product category, video content, and a clear path to purchase. Drive traffic to it through Sponsored Brands ads — use your Store as the landing page instead of a product listing. The Store experience builds brand recognition in a way that individual product pages cannot.
