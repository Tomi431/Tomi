A seller spent $2,000 on TikTok Shop creator collaborations. Three creators. Twelve videos. Total sales: 14 units. The mistake: picking creators by follower count. All three had 200K+ followers. None had ever sold a product in the seller's category.

## The Metric That Matters: Category Fit, Not Follower Count

A creator with 50K followers who regularly posts about kitchen products will outsell a 500K lifestyle creator for a kitchen gadget. The audience is pre-qualified.

Check: has this creator made videos about products in your category before? Look at their last 30 videos. If none are in your category, their audience has no purchase intent — regardless of size.

## How to Find the Right Creators

Do not search by follower count. Search for products similar to yours on TikTok Shop. Then check which creators are promoting those products. These creators have already demonstrated category fit and sales ability.

```bash
# sorftime-seller-agent TikTok tools:
# tiktok_product_detail → see which creators promote competing products
# tiktok_author → search creators by product category
# tiktok_product_video → see actual video performance data
```

## Start Small

Send one product. Ask for one video. Measure views, engagement, and actual sales. If it works, scale to monthly. If not, you lost one product sample — not a multi-month contract.
