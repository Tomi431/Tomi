## 🎯 Final Verdict: DO NOT ENTER Yoga Mats at $10K

The closed-loop methodology produced a clear, independently-verified conclusion: **the yoga mat category is structurally hostile to beginner private-label sellers with a $10,000 budget.**

Three independent data layers converged:
1. **HPI ranking + product deep-dive** → found promising products with real organic demand
2. **Category landscape analysis** → revealed 50.8% top-3 concentration, Amazon Basics anchor, 5,265-review moat
3. **7-member adversarial panel** → three candidates killed by different failure modes (platform infrastructure, ad dependency, unit economics)

### Why "NO-GO" IS a Successful Outcome

Finding out a category doesn't work BEFORE spending $10,000 is the entire point of closed-loop selection. The methodology saved the seller from:
- Burning $3-5K in PPC to climb from Page 3 (Candidate A)
- Running out of money Month 3 when ad costs exceed budget (Candidate B)
- Watching margin erode as FBA fees consume 50% of revenue (Candidate C)

## Appendix: Methodology Notes

- **HPI (Hidden Profit Index)**: Sorftime exclusive. Composite score across "low entry barrier, low ad spend, high profit margin." Full ranking, no hard thresholds.
- **Four-Tier Risk Filter**: Hard Block → Capital-Intensive → Operations-Complex → Trap Signals. Applied AFTER ranking, not as pre-filter.
- **Panel Voting Rules**: GO = ≥4/7 AND Seat 6 ≠ NO-GO. NO-GO = ≥4/7 NO-GO OR Seat 7 P&L VETO. Main agent forbidden from voting.
- **Data Freshness**: Schema synced 14 days prior. All data current as of 2026-08-04.
- **Confidence Labels**: [VERIFIED] = direct API data, [ESTIMATED] = industry benchmark or derived calculation, [ASSUMED] = placeholder needing vendor confirmation.
